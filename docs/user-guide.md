# FinTrack User Guide (FT-68)

FinTrack keeps income, expenses, category spending and monthly budgets together in one private account. This guide covers the tasks available to a signed-in user.

## Create an account and sign in

1. Open the FinTrack address and choose **Register**.
2. Enter your name, email address and a password that meets the on-screen rules.
3. Choose **Create account**. FinTrack signs you in and opens your transaction history.
4. Later, use **Log in** with your email and password. Choose **Log out** when you finish; logout is submitted securely from the navigation bar.

## Record and manage transactions

1. Choose **Add transaction**.
2. Select **Income** or **Expense**, enter a positive amount and the date, and add a short description.
3. Expenses require one of your categories. Income is not assigned to a spending category.
4. Choose **Save transaction**. If the expense takes its category above its monthly budget, FinTrack saves it and displays a warning.
5. In **Transactions**, use search, category/type/date filters and sorting to find records. Use **Edit** to correct a record or **Delete** to review a confirmation before removing it. Export the current filtered result with **Export CSV**.

## Categories and budgets

Each account begins with Food, Housing, Transport, Utilities, Health, Personal, Entertainment and Uncategorized. In **Categories**, add, rename or remove your own categories. Removing a custom category moves its history to Uncategorized; its budgets are removed. Starter categories cannot be renamed or removed.

In **Budgets**, select a category, month and positive spending limit. Saving an existing category/month updates that budget. FinTrack shows spending for that calendar month and the amount remaining; spending above the limit is shown as a negative remaining amount.

## Dashboard and comparisons

The **Dashboard** shows monthly income, expenses, net cash flow, spending by category and budget warnings. Its spending insight calls out the highest-spend category and compares the current month through today with the same dates last month; completed months are compared in full. Choose a month to review another period. The category chart has a text equivalent in the table below it, and months without expenses show a clear empty state. Choose **Compare months** to compare category expenses and total expenses between two selected months.

## Recurring transactions

Choose **Recurring** to add a weekly, every-two-weeks or monthly rule. Monthly dates are anchored to the original start day; for example, a rule beginning on the 31st moves to the final day of shorter months and returns to the 31st when possible. Rules can be paused, resumed or deleted. Resuming skips paused dates, while deleting a rule keeps entries already created. Due entries are generated when the history or recurring page is opened; a scheduled deployment command can also create them daily.

## Savings goals

Open **Savings goals** and choose **Create a goal**. Give it a name, target amount and target date. On the goal card, use **Add a contribution** to record an amount, date and optional note. FinTrack calculates saved-to-date, remaining amount and progress percentage from those contribution records and keeps the dated contribution history visible. Progress stops at 100% visually when the goal is reached, while extra contributions remain in the recorded total.

## Privacy and support

Transactions, categories, budgets, recurring rules, summaries and exports are scoped to your account. Never share your password or a downloaded CSV. If you see an unexpected value or an access error, sign out and report the page and action to the project team.
